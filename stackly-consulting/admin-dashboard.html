Common questions

How do I open a new engagement record?＋
Engagements → + New Engagement. A record needs a signed proposal reference, a lead partner and an agreed baseline metric before it can move to Diagnose.
Who approves outcome-linked invoices?＋
Any invoice with an outcome tranche needs the lead partner plus one other partner. Approvals above $50k route to the managing partner automatically.
How is utilisation calculated?＋
Billable hours divided by available hours over a rolling 8-week window, excluding approved leave and internal investment time.
Can I grant a client extra workspace seats?＋
Yes — Settings → Manage team permissions. Named contacts on the contract can be provisioned within one business day.
